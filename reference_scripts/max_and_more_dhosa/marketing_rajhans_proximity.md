# Marketing Reel — Max & More Dhosa House — Rajhans Flamingo Proximity (confirmed-working)

**Reel type:** marketing / customer-attraction
**Client:** Max & More Dhosa House (`max_and_more_dhosa.md`)
**Status:** Worked previously — use as register reference when scripting future MARKETING reels for this client. Do NOT use for testimonials, launches, or other reel types — that's cross-type contamination per Rule 4.

## Why this worked

- **Single spine:** the entire reel is about ONE thing — proximity to Rajhans Flamingo cinema. Every line serves it.
- **One tagline:** the memorable line *"ek var taste karso etle bija var jarur aavso"* lands as a single zinger. The closer ("Max and More Dosa House — Rajhans Flamingo ni baju, Katargam, Surat 📍") is location-clean, not a competing punchline.
- **No audience cramming:** *"Family hoy, friends hoy, couple hoy — badha na manpasand Dosa mali jase"* is ONE inclusive line that names all personas — NOT three separate scenes targeting each persona individually. This is the key contrast with the failed 3-in-1 version.
- **No internal contradictions:** every line reinforces the hook's premise ("movie at Rajhans → eat next door"). Nothing wanders.
- **Voice register match:** casual conversational Gujlish — Gujarati sentence structure with embedded English nouns ("movie", "varieties", "Pizza Dosa", "Mexican"). Sounds like a neighbour, not a brand.

## Register notes (apply to future marketing reels for Max & More)

- **Hook = direct question tied to local landmark** ("Rajhans Flamingo Katargam ma movie jova jao cho?"). Use the actual landmark name, not "near the cinema." The landmark IS the hook.
- **Solution stated cleanly without competing zingers** ("Rajhans Flamingo ni baju j che - Max and More Dosa House"). The solution should be one sentence, not narrated buildup.
- **Variety pitched as ONE listed enumeration**, not as separate scenes per dish. List the dishes inline ("varieties of dosas like Raja Rani, Pizza Dosa, Mexican, and bija pan fancy dosa") rather than dedicating a scene to each.
- **Inclusivity statement = ONE line that names all personas**, NOT separate scenes per persona. "Family hoy, friends hoy, couple hoy — badha na manpasand" works because it's ONE inclusive beat. Don't expand this into three scenes — that's the audience-cramming failure.
- **Closer = location reminder, clean** ("Max and More Dosa House — Rajhans Flamingo ni baju, Katargam, Surat 📍"). No second tagline competing with the earlier "ek var taste karso" line.
- **Phrasing style:** Gujarati sentence frame, English nouns embedded as-is. *"Movie pehla aavo ke pachi"* (Gujarati frame + English noun "movie") is the model. NOT *"Movie joya pehla aavo"* (too Gujarati-formal) and NOT *"Come before the movie"* (English-translated).
- **CTA is implicit, not commanded.** "Rajhans Flamingo ni baju" IS the CTA — we're right next door, come whenever. No "book a table now" / "DM us" / "call to reserve" in this register. Casual neighbourly invite, not corporate ask.
